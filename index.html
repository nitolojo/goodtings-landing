where it began

it started with post-its.

2021. a bedroom wall. one post-it a day, one good ting that happened to me.

a small win. a beautiful moment. something that made me smile. nothing fancy, just proof that good tings were happening.

waking up to that wall every morning kept me in a high vibration. from there, the goals i'd been chasing started to fall into place, almost effortlessly. it became one of the most manifestation-filled years of my life.

not because of the paper. because of what focusing on the good did to my mind.

five years later, those post-its became goodtings. same ritual, same belief, now in your pocket.

that's why an unfinished week is yellow. every week starts as a post-it.
